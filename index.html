09 / FAQs

Chennai Half Marathon FAQs
GOOD TO KNOW.

Race day & BIBs

When and where is the VAMOS x KYN Chennai Half Marathon 2026?

Sunday, 13 December 2026 at Government Medical College MLA Hostel, Anna Salai, Triplicane, Chennai 600002.

What are the reporting and flag-off times for 3K, 5K Fun Run, 5K Timed, 10K and 21K?

The 21K flag-off is at 5:00 AM. Tentative flag-off times for 10K are 6:00 AM, 5K Timed and 5K Fun Run are 6:45 AM, and 3K is 7:15 AM. Reporting times will be shared closer to race day.

Where can participants park on race day?

Detailed parking information and designated parking locations will be shared with participants closer to the event date.

When and where will BIB collection take place?

The BIB collection date, time and venue will be announced one week before race day.

What are the race cut-off times?

Race cut-off times have not yet been announced. The organisers will share them closer to race day.

Will spot registration be available on the BIB collection day?

No. Spot registration will not be available on BIB collection day. Please register in advance through the KYN app.

Race kit & finish line

What will be included in the race kit?

The race kit will include a running BIB and an event T-shirt.

Will participants receive a finisher medal?

Yes. All participants who complete their respective race categories will receive a finisher medal.

Participation & prizes

Which distances can I run?

3K, 5K, 10K and 21K.

Can children participate in the 3K or 5K Fun Run?

Yes. Children are welcome to participate in both the 3K and 5K Fun Run.

Are there separate age categories for prize distribution?

Yes. The published prize information includes Masters age groups 35+ and 45+.

How will the ₹17 lakh prize pool be distributed across categories and age groups?

₹10L for 21K, ₹4L for 10K, ₹1L for 5K, and ₹2L across Masters age groups. Open-race prizes are split equally between men and women; the site lists prizes through 8th place.

Can families and groups participate together in the 3K/5K Fun Run?

Yes. Families and groups are welcome to participate together in the 3K and 5K Fun Run. Each participant needs an individual ticket, available through the KYN app.
